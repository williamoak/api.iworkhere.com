Proposal: Anonymous Worker Intake Portal for JoinAUnion.ca

Objective
Create a simple, anonymous online portal at JoinAUnion.ca that helps Canadian workers explore unionization options without initially revealing their identity. The portal should collect non-identifying workplace and occupation information, then route the worker toward the most appropriate union, CLC affiliate, organizing contact, or informational resource.

10. CLC-Facing Narrative:


JoinAUnion.ca should not be treated as merely a domain name. It can become a national, worker-friendly organizing doorway.

Many workers are curious about joining a union but do not know where to start, which union applies to them, or whether making contact is safe. A neutral, anonymous portal lowers that barrier. It lets workers explore their options without pressure, then voluntarily connect with the appropriate union or organizer when ready.

The domain package also supports national advertising. JoinAUnion.ca is ideal for radio, video, and word-of-mouth. Hyphenated versions such as Join-A-Union.ca are easier to read in print, billboards, posters, and handouts. Additional related domains provide campaign flexibility and defensive protection.

The portal would help the CLC and affiliated unions:

create a single national entry point for workers;

increase organizing inquiries;

route workers to the correct affiliate;

gather anonymous trend data by region, industry, and workplace concern;

support national campaigns with simple, memorable calls to action;

strengthen the CLC’s role as the central public gateway for Canadian workers considering union membership.


The core message is simple:

“If you are wondering how to join a union in Canada, start at JoinAUnion.ca.”
